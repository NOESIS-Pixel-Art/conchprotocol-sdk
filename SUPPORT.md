# Support policy

No public ConchProtocol SDK version is supported yet.

When the Android Developer Preview is published, support will be limited to its
documented platform, package, public API, and integration boundary. Preview
support will not be a production SLA and will not imply support for unavailable
platforms, migrations, identity recovery, hosted services, groups, calls, media,
or application-specific transport and storage implementations.

- Integration support: **support@conchprotocol.com**
- Security reports: **security@conchprotocol.com**
- Licensing and legal: **legal@conchprotocol.com**

Never send credentials, capabilities, private keys, production encrypted state,
or user plaintext in a support request.

Pricing, supported-version lifetime, deprecation notice, response targets, and
hosted-service terms remain pending owner approval and will be published before
they become applicable.
